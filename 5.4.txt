Released: November 20, 2021


Introducing dictionaries
→ Dictionaries represent key-value pairs similar to tables. They are used to store and retrieve information with keys
→ Dictionaries can have a total of 5,000 elements, the same as lists. The sizes of both sublists and sub-dictionaries are included in this cap
» Some actions such as Set Variable: Get Item Enchantments now return dictionaries instead of lists
