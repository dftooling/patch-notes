Released: June 29, 2024

→ Ranks have received some major buffs!

WorldEdit Changes
» Increased Noble WorldEdit limit from 30,000 to 500,000.
» Increased Emperor WorldEdit limit from 30,000 to 1,000,000.
» Increased Mythic WorldEdit limit from 300,000 to 3,000,000
» Increased Overlord WorldEdit limit from 1,000,000 to 5,000,000.
- Removed most block type restrictions on WorldEdit operations.

Plot Changes
» Mythics can now claim 2 massive plots (increased from 1).
» Overlords can now claim 3 massive plots (increased from 2).
