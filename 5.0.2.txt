Released: June 26, 2019

→ This update expands on 5.0's noise actions.
+ Perlin, Voronoi, and Worley noise now accept a seed parameter.
+ Perlin noise now supports 3 fractal types.
+ Voronoi and Worley noise now accept a scatter value.
+ Voronoi and Worley noise now support euclidean, manhattan, and natural cell edges.
+ Worley noise now supports 6 different distance calculation types.

↑ Noise accuracy has been improved.
